# The Brainrot: 2-minute demo video script

Record at https://the-brainrot.onrender.com on a phone-size window (or a real phone). Speak in English or Hinglish, keep it energetic.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|------|--------|-----|
| 0:00-0:10 | Landing page, drag the 3D cube, click for the emoji burst | "This is The Brainrot: turn any situation into a Hinglish comic, and get roasted by AI." |
| 0:10-0:20 | Tap Start, pick a mood (Exam Panic) | "Step one: pick a mood. Six to choose from." |
| 0:20-0:30 | Choose 3 characters | "Step two: pick your characters. Each has its own personality and drawn avatar." |
| 0:30-0:50 | Type a situation, set Rot level to Cooked, hit Generate | "Step three: type your own situation, or let AI write a fresh comic." |
| 0:50-1:10 | Comic opens, page turns, bubbles, sound effects, Brainrot Meter and Aura points | "The comic plays with page-turn, voices, sound effects, a Brainrot Meter and Aura points." |
| 1:10-1:30 | Open Roast Bot, pick Desi Mummy + Cooked, type "I will start studying tomorrow" | "Roast Bot: four personas, three levels. The key stays on the server, so it works for everyone." |
| 1:30-1:50 | Open Dex, tap Play, then Lyrics and Sing | "Brainrot Dex: 13 original characters with synthesized jingles and funny Italian lyrics, with Hinglish translation." |
| 1:50-2:00 | Share card / PNG download, then the repo or live link | "Share your comic as a link or PNG. Built with plain HTML, CSS, JS and a tiny Node proxy. Thanks!" |

## How to record

- Mac: press `Cmd+Shift+5`, choose "Record Selected Portion", enable the microphone, then record.
- Phone: use the built-in screen recorder on the live site.

## How to submit

1. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or Google Drive with "Anyone with the link").
2. Paste that link in the submission form.

## Before recording

- Turn on sound and test the Italian voice (Dex > Voice).
- Run the Roast Bot once so the first reply isn't slow (Render free tier can sleep).
- Hide notifications and the browser bookmarks bar.
